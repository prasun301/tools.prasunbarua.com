About Prasun Tools

Welcome to Prasun Tools, your destination for fast, free, and privacy-friendly online utilities designed for developers, engineers, students, and digital professionals.

Our Mission

In a world where many online tools require server uploads and complex registration steps, Prasun Tools takes a different approach. Our core mission is to provide lightning-fast web utilities that run entirely client-side inside your browser. This means your data stays on your device, ensuring maximum privacy and instant results.

Why Choose Us?

  • Privacy-First: Processing happens locally in your browser. We do not store or track your sensitive input data on our servers.
  • Built for Speed: Optimized lightweight scripts ensure tools load instantly without bloat.
  • Completely Free: All calculators and utilities are 100% free to use with no hidden subscription fees.
