About Prasun Tools

Prasun Tools is an independent web platform providing browser-based engineering calculators and online utilities designed for practical technical and everyday tasks.

Platform Purpose

The platform exists to provide fast, reliable, and accessible computational utilities for engineers, students, and technical professionals. Traditional online calculation resources often introduce unnecessary friction through cluttered interfaces and complex navigation.

Prasun Tools focuses on simple interfaces, efficient workflows, and user-friendly access, ensuring that calculations can be completed cleanly and without distraction.

Engineering Tools

Prasun Tools develops technical calculators built around standard formulas and established engineering principles. These utilities are structured to support professional workflows and technical analysis.

Solar PV System Design

Calculates array sizing, module counts, inverter matching, and battery energy storage requirements.

Electrical Calculations

Processes electrical formulas, power factors, load requirements, and circuit specifications.

Tools and Categories

The platform currently prioritizes core engineering calculations and technical utilities, with ongoing expansion planned for related domains.

Current Focus

Solar PV engineering, electrical calculators, and core engineering utilities.

Future Expansion

Planned additions include developer utilities, document tools, productivity utilities, and conversion tools.

Privacy and Data Processing

Many tools process calculations directly in the browser using client-side execution. User data handling depends on the specific requirements of each utility, and unnecessary user account requirements are avoided.
