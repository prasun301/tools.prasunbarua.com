Ohm's Law Formulas

  • Voltage ($V$): $V = I \times R$ or $V = \frac{P}{I}$
  • Current ($I$): $I = \frac{V}{R}$ or $I = \frac{P}{V}$
  • Resistance ($R$): $R = \frac{V}{I}$ or $R = \frac{V^2}{P}$
  • Power ($P$): $P = V \times I$ or $P = I^2 \times R$
