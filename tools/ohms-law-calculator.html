Ohm's Law Formulas

  • Voltage (V) V = I × R  |  V = P / I
  • Current (I) I = V / R  |  I = P / V
  • Resistance (R) R = V / I  |  R = V² / P
  • Power (P) P = V × I  |  P = I² × R
