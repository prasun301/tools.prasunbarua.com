Electrical Formulas

  • DC Power P = V × I
  • Single-Phase AC Power P = V × I × cosφ
  • Three-Phase AC Power P = √3 × V × I × cosφ
  • Single-Phase Current I = P / (V × cosφ)
  • Three-Phase Current I = P / (√3 × V × cosφ)
  • Single-Phase Voltage V = P / (I × cosφ)
  • Three-Phase Line Voltage V = P / (√3 × I × cosφ)
