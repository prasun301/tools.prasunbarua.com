Electrical Power Formulas

DC P = V × I

Power equals voltage multiplied by current.

Single Phase AC P = V × I × cosφ

Power depends on voltage, current, and power factor.

Three Phase AC P = √3 × V × I × cosφ

Uses line voltage and line current for a balanced three-phase system.
