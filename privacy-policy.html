Privacy Overview

At Prasun Tools (accessible via tools.prasunbarua.com), protecting your personal privacy is a fundamental principle of our platform design. We believe transparency is essential when interacting with web utilities.

To maximize user confidentiality, most tools on our platform operate directly inside your web browser. This design minimizes data transmission to our servers whenever technically feasible.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our platform, we may collect or process certain categories of information:

Information You Provide Directly

Prasun Tools currently operates without user accounts or registration portals. We only collect information that you explicitly provide to us voluntarily, which may include:

  • Messages or inquiries submitted through our Contact page
  • User feedback, bug reports, and feature suggestions
  • Support requests or correspondence

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit and navigate our website, our hosting and analytics partners automatically record standard technical log data. This typically includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device metadata
  • Referring and exit web pages
  • Date, time stamp, and duration of visit
  • Anonymous usage statistics

This technical data helps us maintain operational security, diagnose technical issues, analyze aggregated traffic trends, and improve overall platform reliability.

developer_mode

Browser-Based Processing

A core architectural feature of Prasun Tools is client-side execution. Most calculators, converters, and text utilities perform computations locally within your browser using JavaScript.

Data entered into these utilities is generally processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless required by a specific advanced feature. While we engineer our utilities to maintain high local privacy standards, users should exercise appropriate discretion when processing sensitive technical information.

cookie

Cookies and Local Storage

We use standard browser cookies and local storage mechanisms to improve your browsing experience. Specifically, we utilize browser local storage to recall your preferred visual theme (such as light or dark mode settings) between sessions.

You retain full control over cookies and local storage through your browser settings, where you can choose to block, clear, or restrict them at any time.

analytics

Analytics

We utilize Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics and traffic reports. This service helps us understand how visitors interact with our pages, which tools are most popular, and how we can optimize layout performance.

Google Analytics gathers aggregated data such as page views and navigation paths without tying them to individual personally identifiable profiles. You can opt out of analytics tracking by utilizing browser extensions or adjusting your cookie preferences.

Advertising and Google AdSense

To support the ongoing maintenance and free availability of our tools, we display advertisements served by Google AdSense.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites across the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads based on your visit history.

Users may review Google's advertising privacy practices and opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.
