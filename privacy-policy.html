Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

Prasun Tools operates tools.prasunbarua.com. This Privacy Policy outlines how visitor information is collected, used, and managed when you use our web platform and calculators.

2. Information Collection

We keep data collection to a minimum to ensure your privacy while using Prasun Tools:

Information You Provide

Prasun Tools does not require user accounts. We only collect information if you voluntarily contact us directly, such as:

  • Messages or feedback sent through our contact channels
  • Inquiries or bug reports regarding specific tools

Automatically Logged Technical Data

When you visit the website, standard technical log data is recorded automatically by our infrastructure. This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device operating system
  • Access timestamps and pages viewed

3. Browser-Based Calculations and Client-Side Processing

Prasun Tools is designed around browser-based calculations and client-side processing. The data and parameters you input into our calculators are processed locally within your web browser. This means your calculation inputs remain on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on Prasun Tools servers.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser local storage solely to remember your display preferences, such as your light or dark theme setting. You can clear or manage local storage and cookie settings at any time directly through your browser controls.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand visitor traffic trends and general usage of the platform. This helps us see which tools are most helpful and improve site performance. Google Analytics collects non-personal aggregated metrics through cookies.

6. Google AdSense

To support the development and maintenance of Prasun Tools, we display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this website. You can review how Google handles advertising data via the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

7. Cloudflare Hosting and Security

Our website infrastructure utilizes Cloudflare for content delivery, speed optimization, and security protection. Cloudflare processes standard connection logs to secure the platform against threats and ensure reliable uptime.

8. Future Payment Processing

If paid tools, features, or subscriptions are introduced in the future, financial transactions will be handled directly by secure third-party payment processors. Prasun Tools will not store full credit card or sensitive banking details on its servers.

9. User Privacy Choices

You maintain full control over your privacy settings while using Prasun Tools:

  • Disabling or blocking cookies through your browser settings
  • Clearing local storage data at any time
  • Using browser privacy extensions or ad-blocking tools

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or technical changes. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
