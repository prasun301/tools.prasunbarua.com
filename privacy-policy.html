Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

At Prasun Tools (accessible via tools.prasunbarua.com), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information collected and recorded by Prasun Tools and how it is used.

1. Client-Side Data Processing

Most tools on this website operate completely client-side using JavaScript within your browser. Input data typed or calculated inside tools is processed locally on your machine and is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Log Files

Like many other Web sites, Prasun Tools makes use of log files. Information inside log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click data to analyze trends and administer the site. IP addresses are not linked to personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Prasun Tools uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, record user-specific page visits, and customize web page content based on browser type.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Prasun Tools.

  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad Policy Page.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our privacy policy and agree to its terms.
