Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

Prasun Tools ("we", "our", or "us") operates tools.prasunbarua.com. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and managed when you access or use our web platform and utilities.

We implement reasonable technical measures and limit unnecessary data collection to support operational transparency across our services.

2. Information Collection

We collect information depending on your direct interactions with the platform:

Information Provided Voluntarily

Prasun Tools does not require user accounts or registration. We collect information only when submitted directly by users, such as:

  • Inquiries or correspondence submitted through contact channels
  • Feedback, technical reports, or feature suggestions

Automatically Logged Technical Data

When accessing the website, standard technical log data is recorded automatically by our hosting and infrastructure providers. This data may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device metadata
  • Referring and exit web pages
  • Access timestamps and duration of visit

3. Browser-Based Processing

Many tools process calculations locally in the browser using client-side JavaScript. Specific tools may use different processing methods depending on their computational functionality.

Data entered into local utilities is executed within the user's browser environment and is not transmitted to our servers unless required by a specific advanced feature.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser local storage to record user interface preferences, such as light or dark display theme settings. You can manage or clear local storage and cookie configurations directly through your browser settings.

5. Analytics

We utilize Google Analytics to collect aggregated usage statistics and traffic reports. This service helps analyze visitor interaction and platform performance. Google Analytics gathers non-personally identifiable traffic metrics through cookies in accordance with Google's operational standards.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

To support the maintenance of the platform, we display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other web properties.

Users may review advertising privacy practices and manage ad personalization settings via the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Our infrastructure relies on third-party service providers to deliver secure hosting and content delivery:

  • Cloudflare: DNS management, edge caching, and web security
  • GitHub Pages: Static asset hosting and web file delivery
  • Google Analytics: Traffic measurement and analytics
  • Google AdSense: Advertising delivery

8. External Links

Our platform may include links to external websites not operated by us. We hold no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of external sites.

9. Data Security

We implement standard technical safeguards, including HTTPS encryption and network security layers, to protect data integrity. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute data security cannot be guaranteed.

10. User Rights and Controls

Visitors maintain control over data processing interactions through standard browser capabilities:

  • Disabling or restricting cookies in browser settings
  • Clearing local storage data
  • Utilizing browser privacy extensions or ad-blocking tools

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical or operational adjustments. Revisions are posted on this page with an updated revision date.
